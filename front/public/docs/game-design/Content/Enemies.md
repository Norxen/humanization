# Enemies

## Known Direction

The enemy species adapts to each Cradle era and threatens the systems humanity is rebuilding. Enemy design should reveal behavior, history, and escalating strategic intent.

- AI senses, coordination, retreat, and objective behavior.
- Prototype roster and production budget.
