# Enemies

## Known Direction

The enemy species adapts to each Cradle era and threatens the systems humanity is rebuilding. Enemy design should reveal behavior, history, and escalating strategic intent.

Combat usually presents small groups of dangerous, readable enemies with complementary roles. Enemies should have moderate default durability and faster resolution paths created by weak points, control effects, positioning, environmental interactions, or engineering tools.

## Questions to Resolve

- Core combat and non-combat roles.
- Faction or species taxonomy.
- Era-specific variants and shared readability.
- AI senses, coordination, retreat, and objective behavior.
- Weak points, protection layers, engineering responses, and group synergies.
- Representative roster and production budget by era and encounter role.

## Completion Criteria

Create a canonical enemy template and a role matrix covering threat, counters, rewards, environment, and narrative function.
