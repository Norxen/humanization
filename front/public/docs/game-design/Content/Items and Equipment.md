## Questions to Resolve

- Equipment slots and tool categories.
- Crafted, recovered, repaired, and unique item sources.
- Durability, ammunition, charge, maintenance, and replacement.
- Rarity and affix policy.
- Relationship between era progression and obsolete equipment.

## Known Combat Loadout

The standard combat contract reserves:

- One primary weapon using limited ammunition or energy.
- One dependable sidearm using reload, heat, or renewable ammunition constraints.
- One multifunction engineering tool.
- Two limited gadget slots.
- Limited healing.

Item content defines the archetypes inside those responsibilities. Additional slots require a clear control and decision benefit.
