# Locations and Biomes

## Known Direction

Cradle zones preserve or simulate stages of civilization. Each location family should combine era, function, threat, resources, history, and a recognizable spatial identity.

## Questions to Resolve

- Biome and location taxonomy.
- Era overlap and transition zones.
- Landmark, hazard, resource, and enemy compatibility.
- Reuse limits and variation requirements.
- Prototype and vertical-slice location sets.

## Completion Criteria

Create a location template and matrix linking generation tags, content dependencies, presentation requirements, and narrative opportunities.
