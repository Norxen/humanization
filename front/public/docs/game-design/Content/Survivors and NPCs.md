# Survivors and NPCs

## Known Direction

Every survivor should have a name, role, fear, conflict, and reason to matter. Assignment begins as a clear capability unlock rather than a deep workforce simulation.

## Questions to Resolve

- Relationship and conflict depth.
- Procedural versus authored characters.
- Content budget for dialog and reactions.

## Completion Criteria
