# Player Progression

## Design Constraint

Progression should reinforce engineering, preparation, and adaptability. It should not transform the protagonist into a combat-only power fantasy.

## Questions to Resolve

- Permanent attributes, skills, tools, and recipe unlocks.
- Run-specific upgrades and temporary conditions.
- Respecialization rules.
- Relationship between equipment quality and player mastery.
- Catch-up and failure recovery.

## Completion Criteria

Define progression layers, unlock sources, power budget, reset boundaries, and representative build paths.
