# Player Actions

## Candidate Verb Categories

- Movement and defensive positioning.
- Primary and secondary equipment use.
- Tool, gadget, and environmental interaction.
- Repair, disable, hack, distract, or avoid.
- Healing, recovery, and rescue support.

## Completion Criteria

Define the minimum action set, control mapping, action costs, interruption rules, feedback, and which actions are available in the first prototype.
