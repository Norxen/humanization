# Encounters

## Purpose

Encounters create decisions inside a run. They can consume health, tools, time, inventory space, information, or opportunity rather than always becoming combat.

Combat encounters should normally use deliberate small groups with readable role combinations. Pressure should come from positioning, terrain, objectives, hazards, and resource consequences rather than defaulting to large hordes.

## Questions to Resolve

- Encounter categories and target frequency.
- Telegraphing and avoidance rules.
- Reward and threat budgets by depth.
- How objectives modify encounter composition.
- Which role combinations best test free aim, scanning, evade timing, and engineering control.
- Anti-repetition rules across consecutive rooms and runs.

## Completion Criteria

Define an encounter schema, composition constraints, representative examples, and playtest metrics for choice quality and repetition.
