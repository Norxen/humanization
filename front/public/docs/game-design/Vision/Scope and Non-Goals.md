# Scope and Non-Goals

## In Scope

- Repeatable dungeon expeditions with extraction decisions.
- A walkable, evolving civilization hub.
- Survivor rescue and assignment.
- Resource, knowledge, and era progression.
- A campaign about restoring humanity and uncovering the Return Protocol.

## Non-Goals

- A full city simulation where every citizen has a complete daily life model.
- Hundreds of systems before the central ten-floor loop is proven.
- Loot collection disconnected from settlement needs.
- Permanent failure that deletes secured civilization progress.
- A power fantasy centered on the protagonist becoming a warrior-king.

## Review Rule

New features must strengthen a game pillar, satisfy a production milestone, or resolve a documented open question.
