# Player Fantasy and Experience Goals

## Core Fantasy

Be the engineer who brings humanity back from extinction by understanding broken systems, recovering what was lost, and making difficult expeditions serve a living community.

## Experience Goals

- Enter each run with a concrete settlement need.
- Read spaces and threats before committing resources.
- Feel increasing tension as carried value grows.
- Return to a hub that visibly responds to success and failure.
- Know why a technology, survivor, or resource matters.
- Experience civilization milestones as emotional events.

## Completion Criteria

This document is ready for review when playtest language exists for the first-run fantasy, extraction tension, hub payoff, and the emotional effect of the first major era transition.
