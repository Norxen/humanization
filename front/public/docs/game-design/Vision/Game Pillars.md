# Game Pillars

## Rebuild Through Risk

Dungeon danger only matters because the player carries value that can change the settlement. Hub progress only matters because it prepares the player to accept greater danger.

## Civilization Is Visible

Progress must alter spaces, routines, available actions, survivor behavior, and the settlement's emotional tone. Advancement cannot exist only as larger numbers.

## Preparation Is Power

The protagonist succeeds through engineering, planning, tools, knowledge, and informed decisions. Mechanical mastery should reward understanding rather than raw character strength alone.

## Extraction Creates Tension

Rewards are not permanent until extraction. The choice to leave or push deeper is the central risk decision of a run.

## People Give Progress Meaning

Survivors are characters and capabilities, not population currency. Technology restores possibility; people turn it into civilization.
