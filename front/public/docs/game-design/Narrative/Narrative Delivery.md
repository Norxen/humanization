# Narrative Delivery

## Candidate Channels

- Contextual survivor dialog and hub scenes.
- Archive logs, terminals, environmental evidence, and recovered memory.
- Building changes and settlement routines.
- Expedition objectives, signals, and enemy behavior.
- Milestone sequences for era transitions.

## Questions to Resolve

- Which information may interrupt play.
- Replay, journal, subtitle, and accessibility requirements.
- Priority when systemic and authored events compete.
- Localization and voice-production scope.

## Completion Criteria

Define channel responsibilities, triggering rules, persistence, content format, and representative delivery tests.
